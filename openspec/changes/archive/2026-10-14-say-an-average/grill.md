## Settled at the grooming grill

Carried here so this file stands alone; the Decided line and `CONTEXT.md` § *Average* hold the same.

- **What it is for.** Where a number or a total usually sits: one figure, read beside the graph.
- **Which pages.** A number commitment's look-back and a total commitment's. A tick's, a quota's and
  a note's say none. Taken thin, despite failing *five percent of seven things*.
- **Which days.** Only what the graph draws as a point. A day holding no number, and a total's day
  with no addition, are not zeros. A number held for a day of a gap is no point, so it is not taken in.
- **Over what.** The span picked, not everything since the day kept from.
- **Said, not drawn.** A figure in words. The **Look-back** entry loses "no figure beside it".
- **Across eras alike**, as the trend does. A changed range or target does not split it.
- **Not the trend.** The trend stays a number's alone, drawn and never said. The average neither
  replaces it nor depends on its switch.

## Settled

1. **A total's average says no target.** "Average 95", never "95 of 120". *The target rule is
   already drawn, and an average spanning two eras has no one target to stand against.*
2. **A span holding no point says no average at all**: no figure and no words in its place. *The
   empty graph already says it.* A page with no point anywhere keeps its "No number yet." /
   "Nothing added yet." and has no average either.
3. **"In view" means the span's newest days, not the scroll.** Month is the newest 31 days ending on
   the last day the look-back counts (today, or the day kept until), 3 months the newest 92, Year the
   newest 366, All every day since the day kept from. Scrolling sideways changes nothing.
   *Provable at the seam, and the figure means one thing per span; following the scroll would be
   shell-only and would shift under the thumb.* A span longer than the history simply averages the
   days there are.
4. **One decimal place**, rounded half up, a trailing ".0" dropped: 81.333… is "81.3", 74.96 is
   "75", 100.666… is "100.7". *Enough for where it usually sits, and one rule for kilograms and
   grams alike.* The digits are otherwise said the look-back's way: no thousands separator, a full
   stop before the fraction.
5. **The words are "Average 81.3"**, a label then the figure. *The owner also asked to see an
   average symbol or icon in the label's place*; the layout round draws both, and which is shipped is
   decided there. A symbol, if chosen, is read aloud as "Average".
6. **The walk is three pictures and no `phone:` line**: a number page at Month with its average; the
   same page at Year, saying a different figure because older days are taken in; a total page at
   Month. The empty span is proved by a kit test, not a picture. *A fixed span leaves nothing only a
   phone can prove.* The history is typed through the day screen, as #369's walk was; there is no
   seeding hook.
