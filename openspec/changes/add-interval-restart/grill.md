# Grill — add-interval-restart

*5 questions over 2 rounds, 2026-09-14. One fact agent dispatched, on how superseding, the change
sheet and records stand today.*

The Feature grill of the eighth grooming pass already settled what restarting *is* — see
**Restarting** in `CONTEXT.md`: every N days only, a supersede under the hood, the start date and
the rhythm unchanged, one entry and one row on screen, reached from the change sheet, default
today. This grill settled the Story's edges.

## Settled

1. **Which days can be picked.** Today or any day before it; never a day after today. *A restart
   records something already done late; a future day is planning, which is what a rhythm change
   is for.*
2. **A restart that would change nothing.** Refused, saying the rhythm is already due on that day.
   *A supersede that moves no landing still leaves a removed commitment in the roster for nothing,
   and the person learns the grid already lands there.*
3. **Restarting alongside another change.** A restart is its own act on the change sheet, and one
   save does not also rename or change the rhythm. *Restart keeps the rhythm by definition; a
   combined save multiplies edges while #249 is reworking the multi-part save. Rename, then
   restart.*
4. **Records already made on or after the picked day.** They are carried onto the restarted
   commitment, so they stay drawn. The existing refusal still stands: a recorded day on or after
   the picked day that the new grid would not be due on refuses the restart. *A plain supersede
   moves no record and would hide them, which breaks "one row, no seam".*
5. **A picked day earlier than the commitment's current kept-from day.** Refused. *Before it the
   commitment was not being kept, and after an earlier restart those days belong to the grid it
   replaced; reaching behind that is a kept-from correction, which the glossary keeps a separate
   act. The roster refuses no date, so the refusal is the screen's.*

## Terms landed in CONTEXT.md

None. **Restarting** landed at the Feature grill and these answers sharpen its edges without
changing what the word means.

## Left open

None. Every question the frontier raised was answered. Two edges were not asked because they are
already settled elsewhere: a stopped commitment can only be renamed (`CONTEXT.md` § *Changing a
commitment*), so it cannot be restarted; and the carry-over and the supersede being written as one
whole save is #249's (`save-change-whole`), which this Story is blocked ahead of.
