## Why

Present ideas are kept in a sixth store that nothing protecting the other five reaches: a copy
leaves them behind, a restore leaves the phone's in place, a take-out does not hand their file out,
and an idea change writes no copy at the copy place. A phone restored from a copy loses every idea.
This Story carries the sixth store as the other five are carried.

## What Changes

- A copy holds every present idea the place holds, given or not, seen or not, whatever the switch.
- Unreadable or later-version present ideas refuse a copy whole, named last, and stop the copy place.
- A copy made before copies held present ideas holds none; one holding ideas the app never writes is damaged.
- A restore puts the copy's present ideas back, whole or not at all, and counts them on each side.
- A torn restore is undone at the present-idea place too.
- A copy place and a commitments screen given no present-idea place read it beside the record place.
- Every idea added, changed, deleted, marked given or unmarked on a Birthdays screen writes a copy.
- A take-out hands out the present ideas' file, is offered while they cannot be read, and names them.
- A Birthdays screen that cannot read its present ideas says a copy can be restored.

  a take-out is. MODIFIED — the take-out's offer, its answer and its refusal.
